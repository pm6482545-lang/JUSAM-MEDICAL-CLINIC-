Jusam began with a child who was cared for, and grew up to care for others.

Jusam Medical Clinic is a Level 2 health facility serving Ramisi Ward. It was founded by a dedicated nurse who grew up at Faraja Children's Home, supported over the long term by North South Partnership Aid (NSP Aid), Norway.

That support shaped a life of service. Today the clinic passes it on to an underserved coastal community, so that elderly and critically ill patients with nowhere else to turn are seen with professional care and dignity.

  1. Growing up at Faraja

    Our founder was raised at Faraja Children's Home.

  2. Long-term support

    North South Partnership Aid (NSP Aid), Norway supported the home over many years and helped shape the founder's outlook on service.

  3. Becoming a nurse

    A career in nursing followed, driven by a wish to care for people who have few places to turn.

  4. Jusam Medical Clinic

    A Level 2 clinic in Ramisi Ward (MFL code 34670), with special attention for elderly and immobile patients, mothers and families.

Questions patients often ask.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

When is the clinic open?

We are open Monday to Saturday, from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. You can message us on WhatsApp with emergency and home support inquiries.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form in the contact section. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and you press send to reach us. You can also message or call +254 707 383616 directly.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Our nurse provides wound dressing, catheter changes and chronic illness monitoring at home for elderly, immobile or critically ill patients in Ramisi Ward. Message us the patient's location and the care needed.

Do you accept SHA?

Our Social Health Authority empanelment is in progress. Please message us before your visit to check the latest status and how payment will work.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, M-Pesa and approved local payment channels for consultations and laboratory services.

What does a Level 2 facility mean?

In Kenya's health system, Level 2 facilities provide primary outpatient care close to communities. For serious emergencies, go to the nearest hospital or call 999 or 112.
