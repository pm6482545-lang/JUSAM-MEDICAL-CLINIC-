Jusam began with a child who was cared for, and grew up to care for others.

Jusam was founded by a dedicated nurse who grew up at Faraja Children's Home, supported over many years by North South Partnership Aid (NSP Aid), Norway.

That support shaped a life of service. Today the clinic passes it on to an underserved coastal community, so elderly and critically ill patients, mothers and families are seen with professional care and dignity.

Questions patients often ask.

When is the clinic open?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed on Sundays. Message us on WhatsApp for emergency and home support inquiries.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form below. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and you press send. You can also call +254 707 383616.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Our nurse provides wound dressing, catheter changes and chronic illness monitoring at home for elderly, immobile or critically ill patients in Ramisi Ward.

Do you accept SHA?

Our Social Health Authority empanelment is in progress. Please message us to check the latest status and how payment will work.

What does a Level 2 facility mean?

In Kenya's health system, Level 2 facilities provide primary outpatient care close to communities. For serious emergencies, go to the nearest hospital or call 999 or 112.

What should I bring?

Previous prescriptions, clinic cards or test results, and a list of the medicines you take.

What happens to the details I send?

The booking form only opens WhatsApp with your message filled in. This website doesn't store it.
