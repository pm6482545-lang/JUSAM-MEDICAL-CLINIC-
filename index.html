Our Origin & Mission

Rooted in Resilience. Dedicated to Coastal Health.

Jusam Medical Clinic is a Level 2 health facility serving Ramisi Ward, Kwale County. Founded by a dedicated nurse who grew up at Faraja Children's Home under the long-term support of North South Partnership Aid (NSP Aid), Norway, the clinic embodies a deep-seated mission of empathy and service.

That early support shaped a life dedicated to healing. Today, Jusam exists to extend that same spirit of compassionate support to an underserved coastal community—ensuring that even elderly and critically ill patients who have nowhere else to turn receive professional, dignified medical attention.
