Our story

Life at Jusam, one room at a time

From the front desk to the laboratory, pharmacy and ward, a quick look at where you'll be cared for.

JUSAM REEL
Welcome

Jusam began with a child who was cared for, and grew up to care for others.

Jusam was founded by a dedicated nurse who grew up at Faraja Children's Home, supported over many years by North South Partnership Aid (NSP Aid), Norway. That support shaped a life of service. Today the clinic passes it on to an underserved coastal community, so that elderly and critically ill patients, mothers and families are seen with professional care and dignity.

Why families choose us

Care for the whole family, close to home

Outpatient, mother and child, laboratory, pharmacy and more, in one compound in Ramisi Ward.

General outpatient care

Consultations, clinical examination and treatment for acute and common illnesses, for every age.

Child health and immunization

Check-ups and routine immunization for babies and children. Ask which vaccines are available on the day.

Maternity and antenatal care

Pregnancy monitoring, nutrition guidance and health education for safe pregnancies.

Family planning

Private counselling and family planning services for women and couples.

Hypertension and chronic clinic

Blood pressure and vitals tracking, medication review and lifestyle counselling.

On-site laboratory

Malaria and typhoid screening, H. pylori, haemoglobin, blood glucose, urine dipstick and microscopy.

Pharmacy

Collect prescribed medicines at the clinic on the same visit.

Minor surgery and suturing

Minor procedures and wound care in a clean setting by trained staff.

Observation beds

Nursing care for patients who need closer monitoring. For serious emergencies, go to the nearest hospital.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Ask us on WhatsApp any time.

When is the clinic open?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed on Sundays.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form below. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and you press send. You can also call +254 707 383616.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Our nurse provides wound dressing, catheter changes and chronic illness monitoring at home for patients in Ramisi Ward.

Do you accept SHA?

Our Social Health Authority empanelment is in progress. Please message us to check the latest status.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, M-Pesa and approved local payment channels.

What does a Level 2 facility mean?

Level 2 facilities provide primary outpatient care close to communities. For serious emergencies, go to the nearest hospital or call 999 or 112.

What should I bring?

Previous prescriptions, clinic cards or test results, and a list of the medicines you take.
